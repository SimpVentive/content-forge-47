## 🔄 CREDIT REFUNDS & REGENERATIONS

### Free Regenerations (Included)
- First 3 attempts per image/topic/video are free
- Generator optimizes until you're happy
- No extra credits charged

### Paid Regenerations (After 3 free)
- Each additional attempt: **50% of original cost**
- Example: Image regen = 4.5 credits (instead of 9)
- Video regen = 41.5 credits (instead of 83)
